_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

## Publisher

gomddoc is published by Monolithic Lab SASU, 14 rue de Cambrai, 75019 Paris, France.

## Data collected

None by Monolithic Lab. gomddoc runs on your own machine or server and sends nothing to Monolithic Lab: no telemetry,
no usage statistics and no update check. Your content and your visitors' requests stay on the infrastructure you run
it on.

## Data on the sites you publish

A site served by gomddoc is yours, and so is any processing on it: server logs, metrics, and any analytics a theme you
choose loads. The review annotations feature keeps its notes in the reader's browser (`localStorage`), and they are
never sent to a server.

## Third parties

Downloads come from GitHub (releases, the install script and the container registry) and Homebrew, under their own
privacy policies. The gomddoc.com website is covered by its own [legal and privacy page](https://gomddoc.com/legal).

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to
[privacy@monolithiclab.fr](mailto:privacy@monolithiclab.fr?subject=gomddoc%20privacy).
