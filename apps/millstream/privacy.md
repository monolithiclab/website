_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

## Publisher

Millstream is published by Monolithic Lab SASU, 14 rue de Cambrai, 75019 Paris, France.

## Data collected

None by Monolithic Lab. Millstream runs on your own laptop or servers and sends nothing to Monolithic Lab: no
telemetry, no usage statistics and no update check.

## Data your pipelines process

The items, artifacts, transcripts and events of your pipelines are stored in the database and artifact store you
configure, and stay there. You decide how long they are kept: retention settings expire payloads and transcripts, and
`millstream purge` erases an item and its lineage on request. Secret values are redacted from agent transcripts.

## Third parties

An agent step sends its prompt, with the item it works on, to the model provider you configure (Anthropic or an
OpenAI-compatible endpoint), under your own account and that provider's terms. Millstream contacts no other service
unless a pipeline's actions do. The millstream.dev website is covered by its own
[legal and privacy page](https://millstream.dev/legal).

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to
[privacy@monolithiclab.fr](mailto:privacy@monolithiclab.fr?subject=Millstream%20privacy).
