_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

## Publisher

Mojiroll is published by Monolithic Lab SASU, 14 rue de Cambrai, 75019 Paris, France.

## Data collected

Mojiroll collects no personal data. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it
makes no network requests of its own. Monolithic Lab receives nothing from the app.

## What stays on your device

Mojiroll keeps a few things on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage:

- the emoji sequence you are editing and its output settings, so you find them again on the next launch;
- your recently used emoji and how often you use each one, to fill the picker's suggestions;
- how many exports you have made, to decide when to show Apple's rating prompt;
- the GIF and video files you export, kept in a temporary cache that the app clears on its own.

None of this leaves your device. Deleting Mojiroll deletes all of it.

## Photos

Mojiroll asks for permission to add to your Photos library only when you tap Save to Photos, and it asks for
add-only access. It never reads, lists or changes the photos already in your library.

## Sharing

When you tap Share, Mojiroll hands the exported file to the iOS share sheet. What happens next depends on the app
you choose, under that app's own privacy policy. Mojiroll cannot see what you share or where.

## Phrase to emoji

Phrase-to-emoji translation uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework, where Apple Intelligence is
available and turned on. The phrase you type or dictate is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to Monolithic
Lab. Dictation is provided by iOS under Apple's privacy policy.

## Purchases

Mojiroll Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. Monolithic Lab receives no
payment details and no personal information about the buyer. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

## Crash reports

If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide Monolithic Lab with
anonymous crash reports and usage statistics through App Store Connect. They contain no personal data, and you can
turn this sharing off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

## Children

Mojiroll collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to
[privacy@monolithiclab.fr](mailto:privacy@monolithiclab.fr?subject=Mojiroll%20privacy). Since Mojiroll sends no
personal data to Monolithic Lab, there is usually nothing held about you. You may also lodge a complaint with the
CNIL ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
