_Last updated: 30 October 2026_

## Publisher

Hazereel is published by Monolithic Lab SASU, 14 rue de Cambrai, 75019 Paris, France.

## Data collected

Hazereel collects no personal data. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it
makes no network requests of its own. Monolithic Lab receives nothing from the app.

## Photos

Hazereel asks for access to your Photos library so you can pick the photos for a reel. With limited access, it sees
only the photos you allow. It reads their dates and locations on your device to sort the picker by day and place,
and never sends them anywhere. If a photo is stored in iCloud, Apple's Photos framework may download it to your
device under Apple's privacy policy.

When you tap Save to Photos, Hazereel asks for add-only access and saves the exported video. It never changes or
deletes the photos already in your library.

## What stays on your device

Hazereel keeps a few things inside the app's own storage on your iPhone, iPad or Mac:

- your reels: which photos they use (a reference to each photo, not a copy), their order, Look, titles, music and
  format;
- whether you have exported a reel before, which decides when the app offers the Pro upgrade;
- the video files you export, kept in a temporary folder that the app clears on its own.

None of this leaves your device. Deleting Hazereel deletes all of it.

## Music

The soundtracks are bundled with the app. Hazereel does not access your music library.

## Sharing

When you tap Share, Hazereel hands the exported video to the system share sheet. What happens next depends on the
app you choose, under that app's own privacy policy. Hazereel cannot see what you share or where.

## Purchases

Hazereel Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. Monolithic Lab receives no
payment details and no personal information about the buyer. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

## Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics &
Improvements on iPhone and iPad, System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements on a Mac), Apple
may provide Monolithic Lab with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics through App Store Connect. They contain
no personal data, and you can turn this sharing off at any time in the same place.

## Children

Hazereel collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data at any time by writing to
[privacy@monolithiclab.fr](mailto:privacy@monolithiclab.fr?subject=Hazereel%20privacy). Since Hazereel sends no
personal data to Monolithic Lab, there is usually nothing held about you. You may also lodge a complaint with the
CNIL ([cnil.fr](https://www.cnil.fr)).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
