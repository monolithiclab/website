## 01 — Fractional CTO

Your company needs technical leadership, but a full-time executive doesn't make sense yet — or won't forever. Monolithic Lab takes that role: defining technical strategy, recruiting and structuring the team, designing system architecture, driving the roadmap.

Regular presence (1 to 3 days per week), sustained engagement, embedded in your team rituals. Not an outside consultant who drops in once a month — an operator who makes decisions and owns the outcomes.

**What you get**
- Technical strategy aligned with business goals
- Team recruitment and structuring
- IT architecture and product roadmap
- Day-to-day operational leadership

---

## 02 — Diagnostic & structuring

Your team is growing. What worked at five people falls apart at fifty. Decisions slow down. Information gets lost. New hires take weeks to figure out how things work.

Monolithic Lab maps your processes as they actually are — not as they should be on a slide deck. The bottlenecks, the duplications, the gray areas are identified. And an organization that holds up is built.

Short engagement (4 to 8 weeks), actionable deliverable.

**What you get**
- Current process mapping
- Friction points and risk identification
- Target organization design
- Prioritized operational roadmap

---

## 03 — Development & integration

Sometimes no off-the-shelf solution fits. The value is in connecting your existing tools. A manual process that runs daily deserves to be automated.

Monolithic Lab designs and builds custom solutions: business applications, system integrations, process automation. Everything is documented and maintainable. Your technical team takes over when it's ready.

Vendor-neutral. Recommendations serve your interests.

**What you get**
- Functional and technical specifications
- Development and testing
- Technical and user documentation
- Integration into your existing environment

---

## 04 — Targeted support

Technical due diligence for an investor. IT audit before a migration. Scoping a critical project. Recruiting a key technical hire.

Short engagements for specific needs, with a clear deliverable and a scope defined upfront.

**What you get**
- Audit or due diligence report
- Argued, actionable recommendations
- Project scoping with estimates and risks
- Technical recruitment support
