## Working principles

### No consulting theatre

Monolithic Lab is not trying to impress anyone with the volume of deliverables or the amount of vocabulary used. The work is useful if it improves execution, clarity, and the quality of important decisions.

### What works beats what shines

Good solutions are the ones that hold in your actual context: your team, your constraints, your pace, your level of maturity, and your ability to absorb change.

### Every intervention should leave useful traces behind

An intervention should leave at least one of these behind:
- better clarity;
- a stronger system;
- a more legible organization;
- better-framed decisions;
- useful documentation;
- a more autonomous team.

### Transfer is part of the work

Dependency is not a quality model. If the company is not better able to function after the work, something important has gone wrong.
