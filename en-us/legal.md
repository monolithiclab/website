## Personal data and privacy

This website uses Google Tag Manager, a service of Google Ireland Limited, to load Google Analytics 4, its audience-measurement tool. The Google Tag Manager script is loaded on every visit, before any choice in the banner (or, with JavaScript turned off, an invisible Google Tag Manager page): your browser downloads it from Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and the address of the page you are viewing. This script sets no cookie and does not measure your visit; it passes your choice on to the tools it loads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Quebec, California and the other US states with a consumer privacy law, Google Analytics is only loaded after your consent, collected via the banner shown on your first visit: until you consent, no analytics cookie is set and no data is sent to Google Analytics. Elsewhere, measurement is on from your first page view, and declining in the banner stops it from then on. Your region is determined by Google from your IP address.

In Europe, this audience measurement relies on your consent (GDPR article 6.1.a), which you can withdraw at any time. When measurement is on, Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga, _ga_*) for up to 13 months for audience-measurement purposes (pages viewed, traffic source, visit duration). This data is processed by Google Ireland Limited and may be transferred to the United States. If you decline or do not respond to the banner, Google Analytics is not loaded: no analytics cookie is set and no data is sent to Google Analytics. Only the Google Tag Manager script described above is loaded.

Outside the Google Analytics cookies described above, this website sets one cookie, mlab_consent, which keeps your choice for 6 months. Only this website reads it, and it is not shared with any third party.

To change your choice or withdraw your consent, click the “Manage cookies” button in the footer, then “Decline”: Google Analytics stops measuring your visits from that point on. Withdrawing does not delete Google Analytics cookies already set; you can clear them in your browser settings.

When you submit the contact form, your name, email address, company name, and message are transmitted to Formspree, Inc. (https://formspree.io), our processor, for the sole purpose of delivering your message. This data is used only to answer your request, is not stored on our servers, and is not shared with any third party. Formspree, Inc. is established in the United States, so this delivery involves a transfer of data outside the European Union. The processing is based on steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract (GDPR article 6.1.b). Messages are kept for as long as needed to handle your request, and at most three years after our last exchange.

In accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act, you have the right to access, rectify, and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@monolithiclab.fr.

If, after contacting us, you believe your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL), 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France — www.cnil.fr.
